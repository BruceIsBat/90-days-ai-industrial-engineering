# Datasets & Provenance

In accordance with repo standards, raw dataset binaries are not committed to Git.

### 1. NASA C-MAPSS Turbofan Engine Degradation
* **Source:** NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE)
* **Direct Archive:** `https://data.nasa.gov/docs/legacy/CMAPSSData.zip`
* **Used in:** Phase 1 (EDA & Baselines) & Phase 2 (Predictive Maintenance / RUL)
* **Citation:** A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon, and N. Eklund, "Damage Propagation Modeling for Aircraft Engine Run-to-Failure Simulation," PHM 2008.

### 2. Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) Bearing Data Center
* **Source:** Case Western Reserve University Bearing Data Center
* **Direct Access:** `https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter`
* **Used in:** Phase 2 (Vibration signal processing, spectral analysis, fault classification)
* **Description:** 12k/48k Drive End and Fan End bearing vibration recordings under variable motor loads (0–3 hp).

### 3. SECOM Semiconductor Manufacturing Process
* **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository
* **Direct Access:** `https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/179/secom`
* **Used in:** Phase 1 & Phase 2 (Class-imbalanced tabular quality classification)
* **Description:** 1,567 manufacturing observations across 591 sensor signals with heavily skewed pass/fail outcomes.
